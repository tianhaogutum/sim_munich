# LMU core photo references

Research snapshot: 2026-11-09. **28 modern/detail references + 9 historical images.** Links point to the Commons file-description page, where the image, author and licensing terms can be checked together. No photos are bundled with the game. Machine-readable attribution is in [PHOTO_METADATA.json](PHOTO_METADATA.json).

The catalogue distinguishes outdoor fountain columns from the similarly named interior fountain. A historical image is evidence of its period, not proof of today’s roof, street or planting. The 2024 restoration image should take precedence over pre-restoration green/bronze finish assumptions. Modern thumbnails directly inspected for the measurements in this pack: the five 2017 exterior views, the aerial, central facade, entrance-facade detail, 2021 west square, Lichthof skylight and memorial Totale 1. Remaining entries were checked through their Commons file metadata; their descriptions are reference guidance, not a claim of pixel-level measurement.

Licenses below describe **each image**, not every page or photo on Commons. CC BY / CC BY-SA require attribution and a license link; share-alike applies to adaptations. Public-domain/CC0 statements are those supplied by Commons. Do not assume an official LMU photo is openly licensed.
